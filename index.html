01Short bio

In brief.

Jiani (Elva) He is a data scientist and independent researcher based in Seattle, Washington. As a Data Science Consultant at Accenture, Jiani has spent five years applying analytics, optimization, and AI to forward and reverse logistics for a technology client. Jiani’s research asks how evidence becomes better operational decisions, spanning demand-forecast stability, causal decision support, trustworthy AI deployment, and AI-assisted reverse logistics. This work includes six 2026 papers with collaborators, among them a first-author paper accepted at AIBThings 2026, and Jiani has peer-reviewed more than ten journal manuscripts. Jiani holds an MS in Business Analytics from MIT (Operations Research Center) and a BS in Industrial Engineering from Purdue University.
