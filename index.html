Semantic Signal-Assisted Inspection and Recovery Allocation in Reverse Logistics

Uses semantic signals in return notes to guide inspection depth and recovery allocation when asset condition is uncertain and inspection labor is limited. Evaluated in three synthetic reverse-logistics scenarios.

Jiani He, Dingyan Shang, Yihua Xu, Shiqi Huang, Yan Lyu, Jize Li, Shangjing Tang

DACRI: Decision-Aware Causal Intervention Ranking for Critical Supply Chains

Ranks supply-chain interventions by recoverable net value, using a controlled synthetic benchmark with causal ground truth and explicit intervention costs, delays, and uncertainty. Identifies when adaptive ranking pays off and when simpler structural policies remain preferable.

Shiqi Huang*, Jiani He*, Dingyan Shang*, Yihua Xu, Jize Li, Yan Lyu, Lashimi Muraleedharan Nair *Equal contribution

Read the short bio

Jiani (Elva) He is a data scientist and independent researcher focused on AI-assisted reverse logistics: using information about returned assets to support inspection, recovery, and disposition decisions. A Data Science Consultant at Accenture, Jiani works on planning analytics for a technology client’s data center supply chains. Jiani proposed the core research idea and methodological framework for SSADS, a first-author paper at AIBThings 2026, and is a co-first author of DACRI at ICECCME 2026. Jiani’s six 2026 conference papers also span forecasting, planning value, early warning, and AI assurance. The conferences have concluded; proceedings are forthcoming. Jiani holds an MS in Business Analytics from MIT and a BS in Industrial Engineering from Purdue.
